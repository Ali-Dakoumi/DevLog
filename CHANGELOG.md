# Change Log

## 0.1.0

Initial public release.

- Environment-aware development log insertion for Vite, Next.js, and Node-oriented projects.
- JavaScript, TypeScript, JSX, and TSX expression detection and placement.
- Log, warning, error, info, and table insertion with recognizable DevLog markers.
- Conservative current-file and workspace removal of DevLog-owned statements.
- Workspace safety scanning, opt-in diagnostics, and navigation to protected logs.
- Multi-root workspace environment detection and custom guard configuration.
- CLI checks for CI and dry-run-aware removal.
